How it Works

From email to approved announcement in 6 guided steps

1

Upload Anything

Drag in Word, PowerPoint, .eml emails — or paste text directly. Our parser handles every format.

2

AI Extracts Details

Claude reads the document and pulls out: location, dates, severity, affected streets, and even original sender.

3

Find Affected Routes

Cross-references your GTFS data and your custom appendix to identify exactly which bus routes are impacted — and during which operating hours.

4

Smart Diversion

AI recommends rerouting that respects bus constraints: lane width, turning radius, bridge clearance, accessibility stops.

5

Word Doc Output

Generates a polished, branded RTL/LTR announcement in Word — ready to attach, print, or post.

6

Manager Approval

One click sends a draft to your traffic manager with an approval link. Approved → announcement is dispatched to your distribution list.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to run TransitDivert?
A modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari), an Anthropic Claude API key (~$0.30 per event in usage), and your transit system's GTFS data. That's it. No servers, no installations beyond opening an HTML file.
Does it work for non-Israeli transit systems?
Yes. The app supports any standard GTFS feed (NY MTA, London TfL, STM Montreal, Tel Aviv Egged, etc.). The Department and Enterprise plans include configuration assistance for your specific feed.
Is my data secure?
Disruption documents are processed locally in your browser. Only the extracted text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis (governed by Anthropic's enterprise data policies — they don't train on your data). Nothing is stored on our servers.
How accurate is the AI?
Very accurate at extracting structured data from disruption notices and identifying affected routes. Diversion recommendations are AI suggestions reviewed by your traffic manager — they're a starting point, not the final word. The approval workflow ensures human oversight.
Can I customize the Word output?
Yes. Department and Enterprise plans include branded Word templates with your agency's logo, header, and formatting. The output respects RTL languages (Hebrew, Arabic) and LTR languages.
